# Kavach Knowledge Base: Digital Fraud & UPI Safety (India)

Sources: RBI customer-protection circular on unauthorized electronic banking transactions (2017), NPCI UPI public guidance (npci.org.in), I4C / National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in), RBI Sachet (sachet.rbi.org.in), RBI Integrated Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in). Facts, limits and procedures can change, so users should confirm with their bank or the official portals.

## 1. Emergency: "I was just scammed" (first hour matters)
1. Call **1930** (National Cyber Crime Helpline) immediately. Reporting fast improves the chance of freezing the money. This is often called the "golden hour".
2. Call your bank's official customer care (number on your card, passbook or official app) and ask them to block the transaction, freeze or hold the beneficiary, and block UPI/cards/net banking if needed.
3. File a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in** (or through 1930). Keep your transaction ID / UTR, date, time, amount, fraudster's phone number, UPI ID, bank account and screenshots.
4. Change UPI PIN, net-banking password and email password. Log out of all sessions.
5. Uninstall any remote-access or unknown app (AnyDesk, TeamViewer, QuickSupport, unknown APK).
6. If needed, file an FIR or complaint at the local police station or cyber police station.
7. Do not pay anyone who promises to "recover your money" for a fee. That is a second scam.
- No one can guarantee a refund. Recovery depends on how quickly it is reported and where the money moved.

## 2. UPI basics
- Your **UPI PIN is needed only to SEND money** (or authorise a payment). You never need to enter a PIN to RECEIVE money.
- A **collect request** means YOU will pay. Decline unknown collect requests.
- A **QR code is for paying**. Scanning a QR to "receive" money is a scam.
- Never share UPI PIN, OTP, CVV, card PIN, card expiry, net-banking password or full card number with anyone, including "bank staff", "police" or "support".
- Banks, NPCI, RBI, police and the UPI apps never ask for your PIN or OTP.
- Always check the **name shown** on the screen before confirming a payment.
- Use only official apps from the Play Store / App Store. Keep app lock / screen lock on.
- **UPI Lite** is a small-value wallet-style feature for low-value payments without a PIN. Keep the balance low. Limits are set by NPCI/apps and can change, so check inside your app.
- UPI transaction and daily limits differ by bank and app. Check with your bank or app.

## 3. Common scams and how to spot them
**Digital arrest scam**: Caller claims to be police, CBI, customs, TRAI or a courier company, says you are linked to a crime/parcel with drugs, and keeps you on video call, demanding money. No Indian agency arrests anyone or takes money over a video call. Hang up, do not transfer money, call 1930.

**Fake KYC / account blocked**: SMS, WhatsApp or call says "KYC expired, account will be blocked, click the link / call this number". Banks do not ask for KYC through links or APK files. Visit the branch or the official app.

**Fake customer care**: Number found through a web search or social media. Always use the number on the official website, card or app.

**Remote access scam**: Fraudster asks you to install AnyDesk, TeamViewer or QuickSupport "to fix an issue" and then drains your account. Never install these for strangers.

**APK / malicious app**: File such as "RTO challan.apk", "PM Kisan.apk", "bank rewards.apk" shared on WhatsApp/SMS. It can read SMS and take control. Do not install apps from links or files; use only official stores.

**Wrong-transfer / "send it back" scam**: Someone sends money "by mistake" and begs you to return it. Do not return it yourself. Contact your bank and let it handle the reversal.

**Refund / payment-received scam**: Buyer says "I sent money, enter your PIN to receive it" or shows a fake payment screenshot. Check your actual bank balance or SMS, never rely on a screenshot.

**Marketplace scams (OLX, Facebook Marketplace)**: Fake "army officer" or buyer asks you to scan a QR or pay a small amount first. Never pay or scan to receive money.

**Job / task / part-time work scams**: Telegram or WhatsApp offers money for liking videos or rating products, then asks you to deposit money for "premium tasks". Genuine employers do not ask for deposits.

**Investment / trading / crypto scams**: Guaranteed high returns, fake trading apps, group "tips". No legitimate investment guarantees fixed high returns. Check whether the entity is registered with SEBI/RBI.

**Lottery / prize / gift scams**: "You won a prize, pay a fee or tax to claim it". Real prizes do not require fees.

**Electricity / gas / bill disconnection scams**: Message says power will be cut tonight unless you call a number or pay. Check on the official provider app or website.

**Loan app harassment**: Instant-loan apps that take contact/photo permissions and threaten you. Use only RBI-regulated lenders. Check RBI's list of regulated entities and report illegal lenders on the Sachet portal (sachet.rbi.org.in) or cybercrime.gov.in.

**Phishing / smishing / vishing**: Fake links, SMS or calls imitating banks, couriers, tax departments. Do not click links. Open the official app or type the site address yourself.

**SIM swap**: Your SIM suddenly loses signal and the fraudster gets your OTPs. If your signal drops unexpectedly, contact your operator and bank immediately.

**Deepfake / voice-clone calls**: Fraudsters imitate a relative or boss asking for urgent money. Hang up and call the person back on their known number. Families can agree on a code word.

**Sextortion**: Someone records a video call and threatens to leak it unless you pay. Do not pay. Stop contact, keep evidence, block, report to 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in. If you feel overwhelmed, talk to a trusted person. Tele-MANAS (14416) offers free mental-health support in India.

## 4. Red flags (any one is enough to stop)
- Urgency or threats ("act in 10 minutes", "you will be arrested").
- Request for PIN, OTP, CVV, password or screen share.
- Request to install an app or click a link.
- Payment demanded to receive a prize, loan, refund or job.
- Caller asks you to keep the matter secret or stay on the call.
- Unknown number claiming to be an official.

## 5. Your rights: RBI limited-liability rules (unauthorized electronic transactions)
- **Zero liability** generally applies if the fraud is due to the bank's or system's fault, or a third-party breach, and you report to the bank within **3 working days**.
- If you report within **4 to 7 working days**, your liability is generally limited (capped by the transaction value or a fixed amount that depends on the account type, for example up to ₹5,000 for basic savings accounts and higher for other account types).
- Beyond 7 working days, liability follows the bank's board-approved policy.
- Where the customer shared OTP/PIN, banks usually treat it as customer negligence and decide case by case.
- Always report in writing / through the official channel and keep the complaint reference number.
- If the bank does not resolve your complaint, escalate to the bank's nodal/grievance officer, and then to the **RBI Integrated Ombudsman** at cms.rbi.org.in.

## 6. Where to complain
| Issue | Where |
|---|---|
| Any financial cyber fraud | 1930 and cybercrime.gov.in |
| Bank / UPI transaction dispute | Bank customer care, UPI app "help / raise complaint", then NPCI complaint on npci.org.in |
| Unresolved bank complaint | Bank nodal officer, then RBI Integrated Ombudsman (cms.rbi.org.in) |
| Suspicious call/SMS/WhatsApp | Chakshu on sancharsaathi.gov.in |
| Lost or stolen phone | Block SIM via operator; block IMEI via Sanchar Saathi (CEIR); ask bank to disable UPI |
| SIM connections in your name | Sanchar Saathi portal |
| Illegal lenders / unauthorized deposit schemes | RBI Sachet portal (sachet.rbi.org.in) |

## 7. Safe habits checklist
- Turn on SMS and app alerts for every transaction.
- Use a strong, unique UPI PIN and a different phone screen lock. Never use birthdays.
- Enable app lock for UPI and banking apps.
- Never share your screen during a call about money.
- Do not use public Wi-Fi for banking.
- Keep phone OS and apps updated; install only from official stores.
- Verify the beneficiary name before paying.
- Use a separate low-balance account for UPI if you can.
- Check bank statements regularly.
- Talk to family, especially elders, about these scams.

## 8. If your phone is lost or stolen
1. Call your bank and ask them to disable UPI and net banking.
2. Ask your mobile operator to block the SIM.
3. Report the lost phone and block the IMEI via Sanchar Saathi (sancharsaathi.gov.in).
4. Remotely lock/erase the phone (Find My Device / Find My iPhone).
5. Change passwords and UPI PIN from another device.
6. File a police complaint.

## 9. FAQs
**Q: I received a "you have received ₹5,000, enter PIN to accept" request. What do I do?**
A: Decline. You never enter a PIN to receive money. It is a collect request, which takes money from you.

**Q: Is it safe to share my UPI ID or QR code?**
A: Sharing your UPI ID or QR to receive payments is generally safe. Never share your PIN, OTP or screen.

**Q: I clicked a suspicious link but entered nothing. Am I safe?**
A: Close the page, do not enter details, clear the browser, run a security scan, and monitor your accounts. If you installed an app or entered any detail, contact your bank immediately and change passwords.

**Q: Can Kavach check whether a number, UPI ID or website is a scammer?**
A: No. Kavach cannot verify individuals. Use Chakshu on sancharsaathi.gov.in for suspicious calls/messages and cybercrime.gov.in for reports.

**Q: Will I get my money back?**
A: Nobody can guarantee it. Report to 1930 and your bank immediately; speed matters.

**Q: Is a call from "bank" safe if they know my name and account number?**
A: Not necessarily. Scammers often have leaked personal data. Do not share OTP/PIN; hang up and call the number on your card.

**Q: Does UPI Lite need a PIN?**
A: UPI Lite payments are for small amounts and are designed to work without entering the PIN each time. Keep only a small balance in it.
